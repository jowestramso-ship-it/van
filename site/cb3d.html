The ceiling blocking, attached: adhesive and screws

One piece of CB1, the blocking under the driver side wall-top trim, between two steel roof ribs, seen from the aisle (the driver side wall on the far side, rear doors on the left). Use Next to go through the five steps; drag to turn the view.

Sikaflex-252 (or 3M 5200)the blocking, 1 1/2 x 1 1/2 pinesteel roof ribs3/16 in plywoodscrewstrim 3/4 x 1 1/2 hardwood

Drawn from the ceiling page of the build guide (CB1: 3 to 4 1/2 in across from the driver wall, the pieces cut to fit between the ribs at 21 3/4, 37 7/8 and 58 3/8 in from the rear doors, each rib 2 3/4 wide). The roof is drawn flat and the ribs as plain channels, and the 16 degree bevel on the bottom of the blocking is not drawn. The beads are drawn thicker than they will be. The ribs hang 2 in and the blocking is 1 1/2, so the top bead fills the 1/2 in gap to the roof. The plywood is drawn see-through so the screws show; the slats, the wool and the wall sheet are left out. CB2 on the passenger side and CB3 under the long seam go in the same way.
