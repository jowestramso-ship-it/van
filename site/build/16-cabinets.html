Step 16 of 17
Wall cabinets, doors and the fold-out table

Last of the big pieces: they are the ones most likely to need small adjustments.

The walls lean in about 5 degrees above 41 5/16 in, so the wall cabinets (63 to 74 9/16 in up) stand 1 15/16 in off the wall at the bottom and 2 15/16 in at the top: scribe the backs or fill the wedge, and cut their tops to the finished ceiling at the wall (74 9/16 in, because the roof arcs up toward the middle). These are the pieces most likely to need small adjustments, so fit them last.
