Van build guide

Every part of the build on its own page, from the first thing to do to the last. Use the numbered bar under the main bar to jump to a part, or the links at the bottom of each page to go to the next. The order is a suggestion made from the plan (the same nine steps as the Build order on the interactive plan, with the parts that came later put in their place); the numbers on each page come from the plan's own sizes.

  1. Before you cut: manuals, measurements and decisions
    Do this first. Nothing here needs a tool, but every later step depends on it.
  2. Roof fan and air conditioner
    Cut the roof first: it is dirty, and everything under it is still open.
  3. Side window
    Another steel cut, so it also comes before the insulation and the wiring.
  4. Wiring rough-in
    Everything that will be hidden gets wired before it is covered.
  5. Insulation and subfloor
    After the roof and the window are cut and the wiring is in, before anything covers the walls.
  6. Furring strips, spacer blocks and shims
    Eight strips on each wall, so every plank is held at least every 14 in.
  7. Ceiling covering
    Ceiling before walls, so the wall sheets finish against it.
  8. Wall planks and floor-to-ceiling walls
    After the ceiling, so the sheets finish against it.
  9. The heavy low things: battery and tanks
    Heavy things go in before the cabinets that would block the way.
  10. The gear cabinet and its sliding shelf
    Floor-mounted, after the battery is in; the couch frame rests on it afterwards.
  11. The removable couch: frame, wall blocks and deck
    After the cabinet, because the deck rests on its panels.
  12. The couch bed: leaf, legs and cushions
    Last of the couch: it goes on the deck of the frame.
  13. Shower pan: set it, drain it, line the stall
    Passenger side, rear end: after the walls and W3 are framed and the subfloor is down, before the toilet and the nook.
  14. Wet bath: stall, toilet and nook
    Passenger side, at the rear end; it needs the passenger-side strips and the walls first.
  15. Counter, sink, fridge and the rest of the passenger side
    After the heavy tanks, before the wall cabinets.
  16. Closet and dinette
    Driver side, cab end of the couch.
  17. Wall cabinets, doors and the fold-out table
    Last of the big pieces: they are the ones most likely to need small adjustments.
  18. Finishing and checking
    The last things, and the checks that tell you it works.

To put this online: upload everything in this folder (the files and the folders next to this one) to any web host, keeping the folder structure. There is nothing to install; open index.html. Every page asks search engines not to list it.
