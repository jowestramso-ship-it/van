Step 9 of 18
The heavy low things: battery and tanks

Heavy things go in before the cabinets that would block the way.

The battery (21 x 10 in, 52 to 73 in from the back), the hot water tank (87 1/4 to 101 1/4 in) and the fresh water tank (87 1/4 to 122 1/4 in), then the gear cabinet (two floor-mounted panels, the shelf as its sliding top), the removable couch frame on its wall blocks and the deck lid, the counter and the drawers under it. The shower pan (bought, 36 x 24 in) is bedded over its drain hole and fill-tested, then the stall wall and W3 are lined with the thin FRP panel (the Shower pan page of the build guide).
