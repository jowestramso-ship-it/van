Steps, in order

  1. Deck lid: 3/4 in plywood, 74 x 26 in, on the frame and the two cabinet panels (the last page), 16 to 16 3/4 in up; check its front edge over the cabinet carries a person before the cushions go on.
  2. Deck details: the deck is the lid above, 74 x 26 in. Leaf: 3/4 in birch plywood, 20 x 73 3/4 in, a plain slab; hinge it with a 74 in continuous hinge on the top faces (#8 x 5/8 in screws every 3 to 4 in, a 1/8 to 3/16 in gap).
  3. Legs: cut four through-slots (1 1/2 x 13 in) in the leaf, 3 in from the free edge, about 24 in apart; each leg is a 3/8 x 1 1/4 in bar, 12 5/8 in long, on a 1/4 in pin, with a threaded foot that screws out about 3 1/2 in.
  4. Latches: two turn buttons or magnetic catches hold the leaf folded back while driving.
  5. Cushions: the seat cushion 5 in thick, and the back cushion 5 in thick and 20 in tall in one piece, leaning back 12 degrees; check the seated headroom (3 1/2 in under the wall cabinets, which are at 63 in up).

C. Driver side: bed and couch, side section

By day the seat is at 22 1/2 in: a 16 3/4 in deck with the 3/4 in leaf lying on it, face down (top at 17 1/2 in), and a 5 in seat cushion (it was 6: 5 in keeps a seated head 3 1/2 in under the wall cabinets, which are at 63 in up). The couch was raised 3 1/2 in (the seat was 20, now 22 1/2) so the gear shelf, with the inverter and the two chargers on it, fits over the battery (diagram C4). At night the leaf is flipped out level with the deck, so the bed is at 21 3/4 in (diagram C2). The back cushion is 5 in thick, 20 in tall (to 42 1/2 in up), and leans back 12 degrees into the wall. The seat in front of it is about 21 in deep (26 in couch minus the cushion's base). The couch is a removable frame and deck lid (diagram C5): a back rail on three wall blocks and, west of the gear cabinet, a rail under the front edge of the deck (the small square under the front of the deck) on two posts; over the cabinet the lid rests on its two panels. The bed makes up with a hinged leaf: a 20 in leaf (3/4 in plywood, 74 in long, no rails) is hinged on the front edge of the deck and lies back flat on it, face down, under the seat cushion, by day; the dark strip in it is one of the four legs, folded into its slot, so nothing sticks out. For the bed you lift the seat cushion, flip the leaf out until it is level with the deck, fold down its four legs (12 5/8 in of flat bar plus a 3 1/2 in screw-out foot), put the seat cushion back and lay the back cushion, as one 20 in piece, on the leaf: 26 + 20 = 46 in wide (diagram C2, with the build in C3). There are no rails or slides, so the space under the base stays free (16 in tall, with the gear shelf over the battery). The bed is drawn 26 in deep measured from the planks; the wheel well and battery are only under the part of the bed where the well is, and the well's 7 in is measured from the wall steel (confirmed), which is 3 3/4 in off the body, so it sticks out 10 3/4 in from the body.

C2. Driver side: the bed with a hinged leaf, day and night

Seen from the aisle, looking at the driver side wall. Day (left): the 20 in leaf lies back flat on the deck (deck top at 16 3/4 in), face down, under the seat cushion (leaf top at 17 1/2 in, seat cushion 17 1/2 to 22 1/2 in), and the back cushion (5 in thick, 20 in tall) stands against the wall (22 1/2 to 42 1/2 in up). The couch is 3 1/2 in higher than it was so the gear shelf fits over the battery (diagram C4). The seat is 22 1/2 in high and 21 in deep (the 26 in couch minus the 5 in cushion). Night (right): the seat cushion comes off, the leaf is flipped out on its hinge along the front edge of the deck until it is level with the deck, four legs (12 5/8 in of bar plus a 3 1/2 in screw-out foot) fold down out of slots in the leaf (one near each end and two between them, about 24 in apart, because the leaf is 74 in long), the seat cushion goes back and the back cushion lies on the leaf as one 20 in piece. The bed is 74 x 46 in (26 + 20) with its top at 21 3/4 in (the seat is 22 1/2 in by day, 3/4 in higher because the folded leaf sits under it); from the aisle only the low strip shows. There are no rails and no slides, so the space under the deck stays open at 16 in (the battery and wheel well stay where they are, with the gear shelf over the battery). The legs, the hinge and the slots are on C3, and the flip is animated on the interactive page (click the couch, or open the Bed, from the end view).

C3. Driver side: building the leaf, slots and legs

Section from the rear (wall on the left, 0 is the finished wall face). Frame: the deck is a separate lid on a removable frame (diagram C5): a back rail on three wall blocks and, west of the gear cabinet, a 3/4 x 3 1/2 in rail under the front edge of the deck on two posts; over the cabinet the lid rests on the cabinet's two panels. Leaf: 3/4 in birch plywood, 20 x 73 3/4 in, edges banded, about 24 lb, a plain flat slab with no rails, so folded back it leaves no ridges under the seat cushion. Hinge: a 74 in continuous hinge, 1 1/2 in leaves, on the top faces of the deck and the leaf, #8 x 5/8 in screws every 3 to 4 in, a 1/8 to 3/16 in gap so it folds flat both ways. Legs: four 3/8 x 1 1/4 in aluminum flat bars, 12 5/8 in long, each with a threaded rubber foot that screws out about 3 1/2 in (the couch is 3 1/2 in higher than it was, so the leg is 16 1/8 in from pin to floor; the foot is a 3/8 in stud that screws into the bar's end; folded, only the bar lies in the slot), each lying in a through-slot (1 1/2 x 13 in) cut in the leaf, on a 1/4 in stainless pin through the plywood either side of the slot, 3 in from the free edge; folded, the leg is inside the plywood, so the leaf is flat on both faces. The pin is 3 in in from the free edge, so the leg lands 43 in out from the wall (the bed is 46) and the front 3 in of the leaf overhangs it. The two end slots are 1 in in from the ends of the leaf (the sides) and the other two are evenly between them (the four are about 24 in apart, so the free edge never spans more than that; with three legs it spanned 35 in); each slot leaves about 4 in of solid plywood at the hinge end. A stop (the end of the slot) keeps the leg from passing vertical and a ball catch or magnet holds it up. Latches: two turn buttons or magnetic catches hold the leaf folded back while driving. Shower: the leg at the rear end of the leaf lands 43 in out, 1 in short of the front edge of the shower pan (44 in) with the pan down, and the leaf edge hangs 2 in over the pan; the pan is tipped up anyway when the bed is made up. Loads (rough): two adults (350 lb) put about 150 lb on the 20 in leaf; with the legs down about 75 lb of that goes through the hinge (about 3 1/2 lb a screw) and the legs take about 19 lb each. A 200 lb person sitting on the free edge between two legs puts about 100 lb on each nearest leg. With the legs up the same person is a lever of about 4,000 in-lb on the hinge, which is several hundred pounds a screw and more than a #8 screw holds in plywood, so the legs go down before anyone sits on the free edge. A 3/8 x 1 1/4 in flat bar buckles at about 3,500 lb, so the bar is not the weak point; the pin, the slot walls and the hinge screws are.

B. Driver side: built items

Battery. The 21 x 10 x 9.5 in battery (280 Ah, taken as 12 V) sits on the floor next to the wheel well, on its aisle side, 1 in from the closet (52 to 73 in from the back). The well sticks out 7 in and the battery adds 10 in, so the two together reach 17 in into the van, which leaves 10 in of the bed's 27 in depth for storage. The battery is 9.5 in tall, so it clears the underside of the bed deck (16 in up, after the couch was raised 3 1/2 in for the gear shelf) by 6 1/2 in: that room holds the pull-out gear shelf (diagram C4) with the inverter, the DC-DC charger and the shore charger on it, 1 in over the battery.

Closet. One rod runs along the wall inside the closet (74 to 89 in from the back, 15 wide by 25 deep) at 71 in up, about 11 in long (76 to 87). The hangers sit across the 25 in depth, 1 1/2 in apart. Clothes up to 36 in long hang from it (the shoulder is 3 1/2 in below the rod), down to 31 1/2 in up; under them, starting 1 in lower, are four drawers (1 to 30 1/2 in up, each 7 5/16 tall by 12 1/2 wide by 22 deep inside). One door above the drawers, 14 3/4 by 43 11/16, opens on the bed-side hinge; the drawers pull out 21 in, which leaves about 1 in of the aisle, so open them while standing there.

The bed, closet, dinette and wall cabinets, drawn on their own so they do not cover the wall details above. They were measured from the rear doors earlier; check whether they use the same zero as the wall numbers (about 6 in in from the rear edge).
