Steps, in order

  1. The steps below are a suggested order made from the plan's own sizes. The plan has no drawn, step-by-step instructions for this part yet, so check each one against the real parts before you cut.
  2. Closet: 15 x 25 in, starting at 74 in from the back (right at the end of the couch); the side panel next to the couch is the couch's end panel (26 deep).
  3. One rod along the wall at 71 in up, then the four drawers under the clothes (each 7 5/16 in tall), and the one door above them on the bed-side hinge.
  4. Dinette: two benches (19 1/2 x 28 in, seat 17 1/2 in up) and a table (26 x 28 in, 29 in up); the backrests stand at the ends, against W1 and W2.
