Step 8 of 17
Wall planks and floor-to-ceiling walls

After the ceiling, so the sheets finish against it.

Walls: 1/8 in birch plywood standing floor to ceiling, joints at 42, 90 and 138 in. Seal both faces and finish before hanging (clear water-based finish or hardwax oil), glue to every strip, a screw with a finish washer about every 8 in, 1/16 in between sheets.

Steps, in order

  1. Seal and finish both faces of every sheet first (a clear water-based finish or hardwax oil), before you hang it.
  2. Hang the sheets floor to ceiling, glued to every strip, with a screw and a finish washer about every 8 in and 1/16 in between sheets; the joints fall at 42, 90 and 138 in.
  3. Stand the four 3/4 in floor-to-ceiling walls (W1 closet end, W2 behind the driver seat, W3 shower and fridge, W4 fridge and counter) on the finished floor and fix them to the framing; the dinette backs lean on W1 and W2.
