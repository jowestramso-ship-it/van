Wall marking map

Every built item laid out see-through on each wall, to scale, with a 12 in grid. Measure along the bottom from the back mark (0 is 6 in in from the rear-door edge, the same zero as the plan and the wall diagrams, so add 6 in to measure from the doors) and up from the floor on the side, then mark the numbered boxes on the real wall. Dashed brown lines are the furring strips (S1 to S8); the dashed grey line is the finished ceiling. Positions come from the plan and change if you change the sizes there.

Both walls are drawn with the back of the van on the left, so you measure the same way on either side. (In the interactive plan the passenger wall is drawn the other way round, with the cab on the left.) Out-from-the-wall depths are not shown here; they are in the plan's item list.
