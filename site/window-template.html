The cardboard template

  1. Cardboard (or thin ply) a little over 54 1/8 x 29. Mark the corner points in the table below, measuring from the bottom-left corner: across, then up. Use a level or a square for “up”, so the frame lines come out level. Join the four corners with straight lines and round the right corners at about 2 3/4 in and the left ones at about 3/8 in.
  2. Mark the opening: the bottom edge 2 3/8 up from the bottom-left corner, the top edge 25 1/16 up, the left edge 9 11/16 in and the right edge 49 3/8 in, all level and upright. Round its right corners at about 2 in. The glass edge should be about 3 1/2 above the opening at its left end and 3 1/8 at its right end.
  3. Mark the three blocks, 1 5/8 wide, centered about 16 9/16, 29 13/16, 44 7/16 in from the left, each running from the opening’s bottom edge to the glass edge.
  4. Cut out the opening. Test it against the window (the frame should drop in with the cardboard sitting on the glass face) and fix any number that does not match.
  5. Seen from outside the van the drawing is a mirror image: flip the cardboard over, or turn the numbers around, before you trace.
  6. Hold it on the van: the glass outline must sit inside the stamping’s recess with margin all round, and the opening must clear the stamping’s inner edge. Then trace and use it as the cut line, once the numbers are confirmed.

What to confirm, and what to measure

  • The shape of the glass: your 54 1/8 across the bottom, 52 1/2 across the top, 28 5/8 high on the left, 29 on the right, and 3 1/2 and 3 1/8 for the top margin are in (numbers 1 to 6). Both diagonals (corner to corner) would settle the shape. You said the area around 50 gets wider the further down: I drew one straight lean on the right edge. If it is a step or a bend, tell me where it starts.
  • What I assumed: the frame is level, the glass’s left edge is square to it, and the top and bottom edges are straight. Check the left edge with a square against the frame’s bottom edge.
  • The frame, outer lip to outer lip (20 and 21): across and up. Its left edge is in (13 and 24). And the bottom margin at the frame’s left end (8): I make it about 2 3/8.
  • The blocks (19): I have the left edge and the width of all three blocks (14 to 18). Tape each one’s length, from the frame’s bottom edge to the glass edge. I worked them out from the sloping bottom edge, so if they come out the same, the bottom edge is not as sloped as I drew it.
  • Which edge each photo was: I took 0045 as the top, at the frame’s left end; 0047 and 0048 as the bottom, along the right-hand block; 0050 as the right (top right corner); and 0046 as the left (you confirmed its 7 1/2). Tell me where I am wrong.
  • How far the frame sits proud of the glass (its depth toward the inside): that decides how much room the cutout has behind it.
  • The van: the same opening, measured on the stamping: width, height and corner radius. The template is only useful once you can compare the two.
