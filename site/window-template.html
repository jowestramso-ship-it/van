Side window: template

Drawn from your tape photos (IMG_0045 to 0050) and the full picture (0056). Blue numbers were read off a tape in a photo, orange ones are my estimate from the full picture, and the rest still need a tape. The window is a big black glass panel with the slider frame bonded in it, and three blocks along the bottom edge. The frame is what goes through the van’s opening, so its outline is the cut.

This is a draft

The margins at the top, bottom and right are tape readings, good to about 1/8 in. The left margin, the frame’s overall size, the block spots and the corner radii are estimates. Do not cut the van from this yet. Confirm the orange numbers first, and check the template against the stamping.

The cardboard template

  1. Cardboard (or thin ply) a little over 54 3/8 x 29 1/8. Mark the glass outline from one bottom corner, round the two right corners at about 2 3/4 in and the left ones at about 3/8 in.
  2. Mark the opening: its left edge 8 1/2 in from the left, right edge 50 7/8 (that is 3 1/2 in from the right), bottom edge 3 1/4 up from the bottom, top edge 25 5/8 (that is 3 1/2 in down from the top). Round its right corners at about 2 in.
  3. Mark the three blocks on the bottom band, 1 3/4 wide and 3 1/4 long, centered about 17, 30 1/2, 44 1/2 in from the left.
  4. Cut out the opening. Test it against the window (the frame should drop in with the cardboard sitting on the glass face) and fix any number that does not match.
  5. Seen from outside the van the drawing is a mirror image: flip the cardboard over, or turn the numbers around, before you trace.
  6. Hold it on the van: the glass outline must sit inside the stamping’s recess with margin all round, and the opening must clear the stamping’s inner edge. Then trace and use it as the cut line, once the numbers are confirmed.

What to confirm, and what to measure

  • The glass, edge to edge: across the bottom and up one side. I used the listing’s 54 3/8 x 29 1/8.
  • The frame, outer lip to outer lip: across and up. Also tape its left edge to the glass left edge: that is the one margin I could only estimate.
  • Which edge each photo was: I took 0045 as the top, 0047 and 0048 as the bottom with the blocks, 0050 as the right (top right corner), and 0046 as the left. Tell me where I am wrong.
  • The blocks: tape from the glass left edge to the middle of each, then each one’s width and length.
  • How far the frame sits proud of the glass (its depth toward the inside): that decides how much room the cutout has behind it.
  • The van: the same opening, measured on the stamping: width, height and corner radius. The template is only useful once you can compare the two.
