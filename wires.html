Wire by wire

One diagram for each of the 36 wires on the electrical layout page. Each starts with the chain from the power (the battery, the shore power inlet or the starter battery) through the fuses to the end of the wire, with the wire numbers on the arrows and this wire highlighted; then its whole run on the wall and the ceiling drawings, zoomed in, from the green START mark to the black END mark, with arrows showing the direction. Colour and thickness are the gauge, as on the electrical page (a solid line is 12 V or battery cable, a dashed line is 120 V). The routes are my proposal, drawn straight along the free bands between the furring rails and up the two risers, not measured paths.
