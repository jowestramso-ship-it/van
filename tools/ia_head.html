Ford Transit van: interactive plan

Click a door, table, the bed, the shower pan or the fridge to open or close it. Right-click an item (or use Show or hide things) to hide it, or untick it under Take items out in the sizes to remove it for good from the drawing and the checks. Drag the drawers and shelves, the jar shelf, the microwave, the trash can, the dish towel hook, the lawn chairs, the water tanks, the drain jug, the drain line, the roof fan and the air conditioner (the dotted purple boxes) to move them, and double-click one to put it back: the message above the drawing says right away what no longer fits or overlaps. Change any size below the drawing and everything redraws, including the walkway checks. Zoom the drawing with the + and − buttons in its corner, Ctrl (Cmd on a Mac) and scroll, or pinch; drag it to move when zoomed. Sizes are in inches, measured from the same zero as the static diagrams (6 in in from the rear edge).
