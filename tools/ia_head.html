Ford Transit van: interactive plan

Click a door, table, the bed, the shower pan or the fridge to open or close it. Drag the drawers and shelves, the jar shelf, the shelf over the fridge, the water tanks, the drain line, the shower fan and the air conditioner (the dotted purple boxes) to move them, and double-click one to put it back: the message above the drawing says right away what no longer fits or overlaps. Change any size below the drawing and everything redraws, including the walkway checks. Zoom the drawing with the + and − buttons in its corner, Ctrl (Cmd on a Mac) and scroll, or pinch; drag it to move when zoomed. Sizes are in inches, measured from the same zero as the static diagrams (6 in in from the rear edge). Static diagrams, photos and the full measurement tables
