Inside the van, in 3D

A 3D model built from the plan's own sizes: the walls and the cabinets in a light wood stain, the countertop medium grey, a light grey plywood ceiling under light wood planks. Drag to look around, scroll or pinch to zoom, and use the buttons to jump to a viewpoint or to step along the van.

This is a rendering of a model, not a photograph. The positions and sizes come from the plan. The finishes are placeholders where you have not chosen: the fabric (oatmeal), the floor (a mid grey-brown plank), the fridge (white), the pulls (black), and the cab seen through the open end. The sliding door is drawn open with daylight coming in.
