# Van project rules

This repo is a custom Ford Transit 350 high-roof van plan. The pages are built from the sources in `tools/`; edit the sources, not the generated HTML.

- **Always show changes in all the html files when changes are made** (user rule). A change to the design goes into every page it touches: `interactive.html` (the adjustable plan, from `tools/ia_*.js`), `sides.html` (the static diagrams and tables, from `tools/gen2.py`, `plan.py`, `cur.py`, `ceiling.py`, `couch.py`, `leaf.py`, `shelf.py`, `couchmod.py`, `letters.py`, `trim.py` (the eight wall-trim drawings on the Walls page of the build guide: 6 the top edge on the driver side, 8 the same on the passenger side); `tools/panpage.py` (the Shower pan page of the build guide: nine drawings, the parts and the steps; run by `buildguide.py`, not by `gen2.py`; the liner and drain numbers are also sizes in `ia_core.js`: `linerOn`, `linerT`, `pvcW`, `linerTop`, `linerGap`, `pdrX`, `pdrY`, `headHole`, `valveHole`, `curIn`, so change both; **the shower stall has no end wall W3 (user, 2026-10-30): the back wall is lined with PVC paneling, nook included, and the other three sides are two curtains on one ceiling rail (diagram E3 in `cur.py`); the paneling is ONE solid 4 x 8 ft sheet, no planks (user, 2026-11-02), with a small hinged door in it that carries the shower valve (4 stainless screws, hinge on the cab side; numbers `PVC_SHEET`, `VD_*` in `shelfdata.py` and `pvcSheet`, `vdoorOn`, `vdoorW`, `L.vdoor` in `ia_core.js`; its two door jambs VD1 and VD2 are furring pieces in `furring.py`, `VDJ`)**; the shower numbers shared in `shelfdata.py` (`PAN_W`, `PAN_D`, `SN_LEN`, `SN_X0`, `SN_X1`, `SN_Z0`, `SN_Z1` = the nook, centered on the pan; `HEAD_X`, `HEAD_Z`, `VALVE_X`, `VALVE_Z`, `LINER_T`, `PVC_W`, `PVC_JOINTS` (planks only: empty for the sheet), `PVC_SHEET`, `VD_*`, `CUR_IN`, `TOILET_X`) are the sizes `panW`, `panD`, `snookLen`, `snookTop`, `snookH`, `headX`, `headZ`, `valveX`, `valveZ`, `linerT`, `pvcW`, `curIn` in `ia_core.js` (`L.snx` is the nook's x range there), so change both); the ceiling lights are Acegoo 12 V slim clip-in pucks (user, 2026-11-09: 2 3/4 in across, 1/2 in thick, a 2 1/4 in hole, two spring clips that only reach behind thin plywood, so **the slat is cut away 3 in at each light and the puck clips into the 3/16 plywood alone**; sizes `lightD`, `lightHole`, `lightGap` in `ia_core.js`, and the light positions, 2 3/4, 2 1/4 and 3, in `ceiling.py`, so change both; the plywood seams across the van are on the middle of roof ribs 3 and 5, 59 3/4 and 127 3/4 in from the rear doors, so the pieces are 59 3/4, 68 and 32 1/4 long, `L.plyX` and `PLYX` in `ceiling.py`; **the ceiling blocking (user, 2026-11-09: 'draw the ceiling blocking'; CB1 under the driver side wall-top trim, CB2 under the passenger side one, CB3 under the long plywood seam at 51 in across) is 1 1/2 x 1 1/2 in pine between the roof ribs, flush with the rib bottoms, above the plywood, 21 pieces with a gap from 76 to 86 in from the rear doors for the cables across the van; sizes `blkOn`, `blkW`, `blkGap0`, `blkGap1` and `L.blocks` in `ia_core.js`, `RIBS`, `GAP`, `RAILS` and `PIECES` in `ceiling.py`, so change both; drawn in diagram G, the Walls page's trim figure 6 (`trim.py`), the plan's Ceiling view and the Ceiling page's cut list; the piece names are CB1.1 to CB3.7**); the numbers of the raised couch, the gear shelf and the side window are shared in `shelfdata.py` (the couch is 69 long, 5 to 74: `COUCH_X0`, `COUCH_LEN`; the nook behind it: `CN_*`; the plan's sizes `bedX0`, `bedLen` and `cn*` are in `ia_core.js`, so change both); the furring dots, pieces, furring rail width (`STRIP_W` 3.5: the main furring rails S1 to S8 are 1x4; the window frame and nook frames stay 1x2, 1.5 wide; also `STRIP_W` in `ia_elev.js`; the code keeps the name STRIP_W). **Naming (user, 2026-10-28): the long boards are "furring rails", never just "strip" (a strip is also LED strip, steel strip, corner strip), and the pad, block and shim under them are "spacers"** and spacer block size (`SPC_*`: 1.5 x 3.5, 3 5/8 out; the blocks of S4 and S6 lie on their side, `LYING` in `furring.py`, the others stand up) and the window frame are shared in `furring.py`, which `furrlist.py` turns into `furring-cut-list.md`, so edit those, not the list), `wall-map.html` (`tools/wallmap.js`), `wires.html` (one diagram for each wire, made by `tools/electric.js` with `tools/elcards.js`: the power chain of each wire is the list `CHAINS` there, so change it with the wire list; it is published as its own artifact 'Wire by Wire' and is also a page of the site), `cheatsheet.html` (`tools/wirecheat.js`, made by `electric.js`: the printable one-page list of every wire with its from and to in short words, the boxes (BX#, JB, JB2) named in those words and, at the top, a list of the boxes (the `s` short name in `BOXES`) with the wires that branch off each (\u2190 comes in from, \u2192 goes on to; found by the code from the box names in the wires' from and to words, so name a box in `SHORT` when a wire ends in it), then the wire table; a new wire needs its short words in `SHORT` there or the build stops; also a page of the site, and `SC=<scratchpad>` PDF made with Playwright when the user asks to print it), `electric.html` (`tools/electric.js`, and `tools/elwires.js` for the wires: the gauge colors, the wire list WR1 to WR41 and each wire's route (WR12, WR17, WR26 and WR36 are retired: a wire with `retired: true` and an item with `k:'retired'`, ED14, keep their number but have no drawing, and numbers are never reused); its gauges and fuses are the ones in the one-line diagram and the cable table in `electric.js`, so change both), `interior.html` (the 3D view, `tools/interior.js` and `interior_scene.js`), `tray3d.html` and `valve3d.html` (see the 3D bullet below), `install.html` when its numbers change (`tools/install.py`), and `site/` (`make-site.py`; it holds every page, including the two 3D pages `tray3d.html` and `valve3d.html`, and it also writes `van-site.zip`, the whole of `site/` ready to upload to a web host, git-ignored, with the how-to in `PUBLISH.md`; it stops if a link inside `site/` is broken). `site/build/` is the build guide: one page per part of the build in order (`tools/buildguide.py`, run by `make-site.py`; it reads `sides.html`, `tools/plan_rows.json`, `tools/build_steps.json` and `furring-cut-list.md`), so a new part of the build goes in the list in `buildguide.py` too. Then update every published artifact link (the plan page comes from `tools/artmain.py` and is published with the whole of `site/` as its files; the 3D pages (`SC=<scratchpad> node tools/interior.js`, `tray3d.js` and `valve3d.js` write their fragments to `$SC/in3d`, `$SC/tray3d` and `$SC/valve3d`), and the electrical and wall-map pages come from `tools/artfrag.py electric|wallmap`; the wires page: `SC=<scratchpad> node tools/electric.js` also writes `$SC/wires/index.html`, publish that), and commit and push.
- **References (user, 2026-10-31: 'Give them all different reference points, so if i say 30 you know which one exactly'):** every numbered thing has its own prefix. The interactive plan: DR# the items on the Driver side drawing, PS# the Passenger side, BE# Bed from the end, XS# Cross-section, RF# Bare roof, CL# Ceiling, FA# From above (`VIEW_PFX` in `ia_ui.js`); CK# the Does it fit checks and the red list above the drawing; BS# the build steps. The electric page: ED# driver wall items (two named items: the lights junction box in the couch cabinet, **JB**, user 2026-11-01 'Call it JB', it was numbered ED21 and that number is not reused; and the soft LED strip's junction box under it, **JB2**, user 2026-11-05), EP# passenger wall items, **BX# the wall boxes you wire by hand (user, 2026-11-06: 'Call all these boxes a letter or number'; the list `BOXES` in `tools/electric.js`, with a dashed brown outline on the wall drawings and a table in section 3a of the electric page: BX1 rear doors plate ED11, BX2 the couch box on S6 (ED10, ED19), BX3 dinette table outlet ED15, BX4 the dinette back wall box W2 (ED12, ED22), BX5 fridge and microwave outlets (EP3, EP4; 55 along and 60 up), BX6 selector and counter outlet (EP9, EP15; the end of circuit A, fed by WR41 from BX8), BX7 heater outlet EP7, BX8 TV outlet EP6 (92 along and 60 up, above the kitchen nook, behind the TV), BX9 the switch plate EP11 and EP14 next to the sliding door; JB and JB2 keep their names; a device in a box has `bx:'BX#'` in its item; never renumber, a new box takes the next number)**, PL# plumbing, **CB# the ceiling blocking rails (CB1 driver side wall top, CB2 passenger side wall top, CB3 under the long plywood seam; pieces CB1.1 to CB3.7)**, **PW# the six ceiling plywood pieces in the order they go up (PW1 rear 59 3/4 x 48 with the two openings, PW2 rear strip 59 3/4 x 14, PW3 68 x 48, PW4 68 x 14, PW5 cab end 32 1/4 x 48, PW6 cab end strip 32 1/4 x 14; diagram H on the ceiling page, `_hdiag` in `ceiling.py`, the plan's Ceiling view labels)**, WR# wires, EF# the diagrams themselves (EF1 to EF25 on the electric page, each with a black tag by its figure: EF1 one-line, EF2 selector, EF3 divider panel, EF4 closet end panel, EF5 board on the tray, EF6 driver wall, EF7 passenger wall, EF8 retired (it was the ceiling by type, a duplicate of EF12), EF9 to EF12 the one-line, driver wall, passenger wall and ceiling by gauge; EF13 the soft LED strip wired (fuse block, JB, the slide dimmer on W2, the three strips); EF14 the rear doors plate ED11 wired (a lever connector, the jumpers, WR21, WR22, WR23); EF15 the lights junction box JB wired (two 5 port lever connectors, five pairs); EF16 the strip junction box JB2 wired (three lever connectors); EF17 the lights junction box JB with 3 port connectors and jumpers; EF18 the strip junction box JB2 with 3 port connectors and jumpers; EF19 the passenger switch plate BX9 (EP11 and EP14) wired; EF20 the TV outlet box BX8 (EP6) wired; EF21 the couch box BX2 (ED10, ED19) wired; EF22 the selector and counter outlet box BX6 (EP9, EP15) wired; EF23 the selector's pins: crimping the terminals and shrinking the boots; EF24 the dinette back wall box BX4 (ED12, ED22) wired; EF25 the dinette lights ED9 and ED8 wired (WR25 splits to the two lights in 3 port lever connectors); never renumber, a new diagram takes the next number); on the wire cards of `wires.html` each diagram is tagged WR# A (power chain), B and C (the route drawings). Other pages keep their own letters: furring rails S#, nook and window frames V# and J#, the valve door jambs VD1 and VD2 (the cut list's E08 and E09), steel pans P#, the cut list's D## and P##. A bare number from the user is a distance (inches along the wall from the back mark, which is 6 in in from the rear-door edge; the ceiling diagram's numbers are from the rear-door edge) unless it carries a prefix; if it is unclear, ask which one. New numbered things get a new prefix, never a bare number. **Naming (user, 2026-11-06: 'Don't use riser', 'why are you even using the word if its not a start or end point?'): never the word "riser"; the cable channels inside the walls are the "driver wall run" (at 76 1/2 in on the wall maps, 82 1/2 on the ceiling diagram) and the "passenger wall run" (at 73, 79 on the ceiling diagram), described, not named by a letter; a wire starts and ends at a device or box, never at a wall run (user, 2026-11-06: 'wall run is better but always have a start and end point. those should never be wall run or risers'): so each cable is one wire from device to device; the circuit B/D cable is WR27, from the AC panel to the selector box, and there is no circuit C any more: the fridge outlet is on circuit A (WR30 couch outlet to TV outlet, then WR37 TV outlet to fridge outlet and WR41 TV outlet to counter outlet: the TV box BX8 is where circuit A branches).**
- **The 3D pages are kept up to date again (user, 2026-11-03: 'update all the 3D images'; the holds of 2026-10-27 and 2026-11-01 are lifted).** A change to the design goes into the 3D pages it touches too: the van interior (`tools/interior.js` reads the plan in a headless browser and passes the numbers; `tools/interior_scene.js` draws them; `interior.html`, also in `site/`; published as 'Van Interior 3D' with `SC=<scratchpad> node tools/interior.js`, which writes `$SC/in3d/index.html`), the pull-out tray (`tools/tray3d.js` from `TRAY_PARTS` in `tools/elhub.js`: the tray, the Class T and shunt on the closet's end panel, the TS-30, AC panel and fuse block on the divider panel; `tray3d.html`; artifact 'Pull-Out Tray in 3D', fragment `$SC/tray3d/index.html`) and the shower valve (`tools/valve3d.js`: the PVC sheet, the nook, the valve door with its gasket, hinge, four screws and jambs VD1 and VD2, the valve, the elbows, the PEX lines and the head above the valve; `valve3d.html`; artifact 'Shower Valve Access', fragment `$SC/valve3d/index.html`). `build.sh` runs all three. Each writes a local-three.js copy for stills when `SC` is set (`$SC/three3d/*_local.html`, three.js in `$SC/three3d/node_modules`). If the user says to stop the 3D again, record it here.
- Rebuild with `sh tools/build.sh` (do not pipe it through head).
- Add an entry to `van-notes.md` for every change.
- Never make the repo public: the top-level `photos/` has GPS data. Only `site/` (photos with the metadata stripped) is meant to be published.
- Commit on the working branch and push there; do not open pull requests unless asked.
