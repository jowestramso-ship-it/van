# Van project rules

This repo is a custom Ford Transit 350 high-roof van plan. The pages are built from the sources in `tools/`; edit the sources, not the generated HTML.

- **Always show changes in all the html files when changes are made** (user rule). A change to the design goes into every page it touches: `interactive.html` (the adjustable plan, from `tools/ia_*.js`), `sides.html` (the static diagrams and tables, from `tools/gen2.py`, `plan.py`, `cur.py`, `ceiling.py`, `couch.py`, `leaf.py`, `shelf.py`, `couchmod.py`, `letters.py`, `trim.py` (the seven wall-trim drawings on the Walls page of the build guide); `tools/panpage.py` (the Shower pan page of the build guide: nine drawings, the parts and the steps; run by `buildguide.py`, not by `gen2.py`; the liner and drain numbers are also sizes in `ia_core.js`: `linerOn`, `linerT`, `pvcW`, `linerTop`, `linerGap`, `pdrX`, `pdrY`, `headHole`, `valveHole`, `curIn`, so change both; **the shower stall has no end wall W3 (user, 2026-10-30): the back wall is lined with PVC paneling, nook included, and the other three sides are two curtains on one ceiling rail (diagram E3 in `cur.py`); the paneling is ONE solid 4 x 8 ft sheet, no planks (user, 2026-11-02), with a small hinged door in it that carries the shower valve (4 stainless screws, hinge on the cab side; numbers `PVC_SHEET`, `VD_*` in `shelfdata.py` and `pvcSheet`, `vdoorOn`, `vdoorW`, `L.vdoor` in `ia_core.js`; its two door jambs VD1 and VD2 are furring pieces in `furring.py`, `VDJ`)**; the shower numbers shared in `shelfdata.py` (`PAN_W`, `PAN_D`, `SN_LEN`, `SN_X0`, `SN_X1`, `SN_Z0`, `SN_Z1` = the nook, centered on the pan; `HEAD_X`, `HEAD_Z`, `VALVE_X`, `VALVE_Z`, `LINER_T`, `PVC_W`, `PVC_JOINTS` (planks only: empty for the sheet), `PVC_SHEET`, `VD_*`, `CUR_IN`, `TOILET_X`) are the sizes `panW`, `panD`, `snookLen`, `snookTop`, `snookH`, `headX`, `headZ`, `valveX`, `valveZ`, `linerT`, `pvcW`, `curIn` in `ia_core.js` (`L.snx` is the nook's x range there), so change both); the numbers of the raised couch, the gear shelf and the side window are shared in `shelfdata.py` (the couch is 69 long, 5 to 74: `COUCH_X0`, `COUCH_LEN`; the nook behind it: `CN_*`; the plan's sizes `bedX0`, `bedLen` and `cn*` are in `ia_core.js`, so change both); the furring dots, pieces, furring rail width (`STRIP_W` 3.5: the main furring rails S1 to S8 are 1x4; the window frame and nook frames stay 1x2, 1.5 wide; also `STRIP_W` in `ia_elev.js`; the code keeps the name STRIP_W). **Naming (user, 2026-10-28): the long boards are "furring rails", never just "strip" (a strip is also LED strip, steel strip, corner strip), and the pad, block and shim under them are "spacers"** and spacer block size (`SPC_*`: 1.5 x 3.5, 3 5/8 out; the blocks of S4 and S6 lie on their side, `LYING` in `furring.py`, the others stand up) and the window frame are shared in `furring.py`, which `furrlist.py` turns into `furring-cut-list.md`, so edit those, not the list), `wall-map.html` (`tools/wallmap.js`), `wires.html` (one diagram for each wire, made by `tools/electric.js` with `tools/elcards.js`: the power chain of each wire is the list `CHAINS` there, so change it with the wire list; it is published as its own artifact 'Wire by Wire' and is also a page of the site), `electric.html` (`tools/electric.js`, and `tools/elwires.js` for the wires: the gauge colors, the wire list WR1 to WR36 and each wire's route; its gauges and fuses are the ones in the one-line diagram and the cable table in `electric.js`, so change both), `interior.html` (the 3D view, `tools/interior.js` and `interior_scene.js`), `tray3d.html` and `valve3d.html` (see the 3D bullet below), `install.html` when its numbers change (`tools/install.py`), and `site/` (`make-site.py`). `site/build/` is the build guide: one page per part of the build in order (`tools/buildguide.py`, run by `make-site.py`; it reads `sides.html`, `tools/plan_rows.json`, `tools/build_steps.json` and `furring-cut-list.md`), so a new part of the build goes in the list in `buildguide.py` too. Then update every published artifact link (the plan page comes from `tools/artmain.py` and is published with the whole of `site/` as its files; the 3D pages (`SC=<scratchpad> node tools/interior.js`, `tray3d.js` and `valve3d.js` write their fragments to `$SC/in3d`, `$SC/tray3d` and `$SC/valve3d`), and the electrical and wall-map pages come from `tools/artfrag.py electric|wallmap`; the wires page: `SC=<scratchpad> node tools/electric.js` also writes `$SC/wires/index.html`, publish that), and commit and push.
- **References (user, 2026-10-31: 'Give them all different reference points, so if i say 30 you know which one exactly'):** every numbered thing has its own prefix. The interactive plan: DR# the items on the Driver side drawing, PS# the Passenger side, BE# Bed from the end, XS# Cross-section, RF# Bare roof, CL# Ceiling, FA# From above (`VIEW_PFX` in `ia_ui.js`); CK# the Does it fit checks and the red list above the drawing; BS# the build steps. The electric page: ED# driver wall items (the lights junction box in the couch cabinet is the one named item: **JB**, user 2026-11-01 'Call it JB'; it was numbered ED21 and that number is not reused), EP# passenger wall items, PL# plumbing, WR# wires, EF# the diagrams themselves (EF1 to EF12 on the electric page, each with a black tag by its figure: EF1 one-line, EF2 selector, EF3 divider panel, EF4 closet end panel, EF5 board on the tray, EF6 driver wall, EF7 passenger wall, EF8 retired (it was the ceiling by type, a duplicate of EF12), EF9 to EF12 the one-line, driver wall, passenger wall and ceiling by gauge; never renumber, a new diagram takes the next number); on the wire cards of `wires.html` each diagram is tagged WR# A (power chain), B and C (the route drawings). Other pages keep their own letters: furring rails S#, nook and window frames V# and J#, the valve door jambs VD1 and VD2 (the cut list's E08 and E09), steel pans P#, the cut list's D## and P##. A bare number from the user is a distance (inches along the wall from the back mark, which is 6 in in from the rear-door edge; the ceiling diagram's numbers are from the rear-door edge) unless it carries a prefix; if it is unclear, ask which one. New numbered things get a new prefix, never a bare number.
- **The 3D pages are kept up to date again (user, 2026-11-03: 'update all the 3D images'; the holds of 2026-10-27 and 2026-11-01 are lifted).** A change to the design goes into the 3D pages it touches too: the van interior (`tools/interior.js` reads the plan in a headless browser and passes the numbers; `tools/interior_scene.js` draws them; `interior.html`, also in `site/`; published as 'Van Interior 3D' with `SC=<scratchpad> node tools/interior.js`, which writes `$SC/in3d/index.html`), the pull-out tray (`tools/tray3d.js` from `TRAY_PARTS` in `tools/elhub.js`: the tray, the Class T and shunt on the closet's end panel, the TS-30, AC panel and fuse block on the divider panel; `tray3d.html`; artifact 'Pull-Out Tray in 3D', fragment `$SC/tray3d/index.html`) and the shower valve (`tools/valve3d.js`: the PVC sheet, the nook, the valve door with its gasket, hinge, four screws and jambs VD1 and VD2, the valve, the elbows, the PEX lines and the head above the valve; `valve3d.html`; artifact 'Shower Valve Access', fragment `$SC/valve3d/index.html`). `build.sh` runs all three. Each writes a local-three.js copy for stills when `SC` is set (`$SC/three3d/*_local.html`, three.js in `$SC/three3d/node_modules`). If the user says to stop the 3D again, record it here.
- Rebuild with `sh tools/build.sh` (do not pipe it through head).
- Add an entry to `van-notes.md` for every change.
- Never make the repo public: the top-level `photos/` has GPS data. Only `site/` (photos with the metadata stripped) is meant to be published.
- Commit on the working branch and push there; do not open pull requests unless asked.
