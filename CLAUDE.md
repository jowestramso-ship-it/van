# Van project rules

This repo is a custom Ford Transit 350 high-roof van plan. The pages are built from the sources in `tools/`; edit the sources, not the generated HTML.

- **Always show changes in all the html files when changes are made** (user rule). A change to the design goes into every page it touches: `interactive.html` (the adjustable plan, from `tools/ia_*.js`), `sides.html` (the static diagrams and tables, from `tools/gen2.py`, `plan.py`, `cur.py`, `ceiling.py`, `couch.py`, `leaf.py`, `shelf.py`, `couchmod.py`, `letters.py`, `trim.py` (the seven wall-trim drawings on the Walls page of the build guide); `tools/panpage.py` (the Shower pan page of the build guide: nine drawings, the parts and the steps; run by `buildguide.py`, not by `gen2.py`; the liner and drain numbers are also sizes in `ia_core.js`: `linerOn`, `linerT`, `pvcW`, `linerTop`, `linerGap`, `pdrX`, `pdrY`, `headHole`, `valveHole`, `curIn`, so change both; **the shower stall has no end wall W3 (user, 2026-10-30): the back wall is lined with PVC paneling, 4 planks on end, nook included, and the other three sides are two curtains on one ceiling rail (diagram E3 in `cur.py`)**; the shower numbers shared in `shelfdata.py` (`PAN_W`, `PAN_D`, `SN_LEN`, `SN_X0`, `SN_X1`, `SN_Z0`, `SN_Z1` = the nook, centered on the pan; `HEAD_X`, `HEAD_Z`, `VALVE_X`, `VALVE_Z`, `LINER_T`, `PVC_W`, `PVC_JOINTS`, `CUR_IN`, `TOILET_X`) are the sizes `panW`, `panD`, `snookLen`, `snookTop`, `snookH`, `headX`, `headZ`, `valveX`, `valveZ`, `linerT`, `pvcW`, `curIn` in `ia_core.js` (`L.snx` is the nook's x range there), so change both); the numbers of the raised couch, the gear shelf and the side window are shared in `shelfdata.py` (the couch is 69 long, 5 to 74: `COUCH_X0`, `COUCH_LEN`; the nook behind it: `CN_*`; the plan's sizes `bedX0`, `bedLen` and `cn*` are in `ia_core.js`, so change both); the furring dots, pieces, furring rail width (`STRIP_W` 3.5: the main furring rails S1 to S8 are 1x4; the window frame and nook frames stay 1x2, 1.5 wide; also `STRIP_W` in `ia_elev.js`; the code keeps the name STRIP_W). **Naming (user, 2026-10-28): the long boards are "furring rails", never just "strip" (a strip is also LED strip, steel strip, corner strip), and the pad, block and shim under them are "spacers"** and spacer block size (`SPC_*`: 1.5 x 3.5, 3 5/8 out; the blocks of S4 and S6 lie on their side, `LYING` in `furring.py`, the others stand up) and the window frame are shared in `furring.py`, which `furrlist.py` turns into `furring-cut-list.md`, so edit those, not the list), `wall-map.html` (`tools/wallmap.js`), `wires.html` (one diagram for each wire, made by `tools/electric.js` with `tools/elcards.js`: the power chain of each wire is the list `CHAINS` there, so change it with the wire list; it is published as its own artifact 'Wire by Wire' and is also a page of the site), `electric.html` (`tools/electric.js`, and `tools/elwires.js` for the wires: the gauge colors, the wire list WR1 to WR34 and each wire's route; its gauges and fuses are the ones in the one-line diagram and the cable table in `electric.js`, so change both), `interior.html` (the 3D view, `tools/interior.js` and `interior_scene.js`) [ON HOLD, see the 3D bullet below], `install.html` when its numbers change (`tools/install.py`), and `site/` (`make-site.py`). `site/build/` is the build guide: one page per part of the build in order (`tools/buildguide.py`, run by `make-site.py`; it reads `sides.html`, `tools/plan_rows.json`, `tools/build_steps.json` and `furring-cut-list.md`), so a new part of the build goes in the list in `buildguide.py` too. Then update every published artifact link (the plan page comes from `tools/artmain.py` and is published with the whole of `site/` as its files; the 3D (skip it while it is on hold), electrical and wall-map pages come from `node tools/interior.js` with `SC` set to the scratchpad, and `tools/artfrag.py electric|wallmap`; the wires page: `SC=<scratchpad> node tools/electric.js` also writes `$SC/wires/index.html`, publish that), and commit and push.
- **References (user, 2026-10-31: 'Give them all different reference points, so if i say 30 you know which one exactly'):** every numbered thing has its own prefix. The interactive plan: DR# the items on the Driver side drawing, PS# the Passenger side, BE# Bed from the end, XS# Cross-section, RF# Bare roof, CL# Ceiling, FA# From above (`VIEW_PFX` in `ia_ui.js`); CK# the Does it fit checks and the red list above the drawing; BS# the build steps. The electric page: ED# driver wall items, EP# passenger wall items, PL# plumbing, WR# wires, EF# the diagrams themselves (EF1 to EF12 on the electric page, each with a black tag by its figure: EF1 one-line, EF2 selector, EF3 divider panel, EF4 closet end panel, EF5 board on the tray, EF6 driver wall, EF7 passenger wall, EF8 retired (it was the ceiling by type, a duplicate of EF12), EF9 to EF12 the one-line, driver wall, passenger wall and ceiling by gauge; never renumber, a new diagram takes the next number); on the wire cards of `wires.html` each diagram is tagged WR# A (power chain), B and C (the route drawings). Other pages keep their own letters: furring rails S#, nook and window frames V# and J#, steel pans P#, the cut list's D## and P##. A bare number from the user is a distance (inches along the wall from the back mark, which is 6 in in from the rear-door edge; the ceiling diagram's numbers are from the rear-door edge) unless it carries a prefix; if it is unclear, ask which one. New numbered things get a new prefix, never a bare number.
- **The 3D of the pull-out tray is on hold too (user, 2026-11-01: first 'can you show me a 3D of the pull-out tray and where the other parts would go?', then 'ok stop doing the 3d version for now').** It exists as its own page, `tray3d.html` from `tools/tray3d.js` (three.js r128 from cdnjs; the tray parts come from `TRAY_PARTS` in `tools/elhub.js`), published as the artifact 'Pull-Out Tray in 3D'. Until the user says to continue: do NOT edit `tools/tray3d.js`, do NOT regenerate or commit `tray3d.html` (`build.sh` no longer runs it) and do NOT republish that artifact. As published it shows the TS-30, the AC panel and the fuse block on the driver wall; the plan now has them on the divider panel (section 1b of the electric page), so it is out of date. Changing `TRAY_PARTS` in `elhub.js` changes the flat drawings only.
- **The 3D view is on hold again (2026-10-27).** The user said 'Don't do any 3D diagrams until I say' (earlier: 'Leave 3d view until I say to make it', then 'show me how the lift-out rail will work via 3d'). Until the user says to make it: do NOT edit `tools/interior.js` or `tools/interior_scene.js`, do NOT commit a regenerated `interior.html` or `site/interior.html` (`sh tools/build.sh` rewrites both, so right after a build run `git checkout -- interior.html site/interior.html`), and do NOT republish the 3D artifact. Every other page still follows the rule above. The 3D view as it stands shows the couch at 17 1/2 in with the gear cabinet, the three Play buttons and the nook with the old 1 1/2 in lip; it does not show the 1 in raise (couch at 18 1/2), the 3/4 x 1 front rail across the cabinet or the nook bungee cord. When the user says to make it again, bring it up to date from `interior.js` (it already passes `gcab`, `cblk`, `cmod`; `cmod.fr` is the rail across the cabinet) and `interior_scene.js` (rail across the cabinet in `frameG`, leg foot length `DT - .625 - 12.625 + .2`, the nook cord from `L.sn.cord`).
- Rebuild with `sh tools/build.sh` (do not pipe it through head).
- Add an entry to `van-notes.md` for every change.
- Never make the repo public: the top-level `photos/` has GPS data. Only `site/` (photos with the metadata stripped) is meant to be published.
- Commit on the working branch and push there; do not open pull requests unless asked.
