# Ford Transit van conversion: notes

Interior: 160 in long, 68 in wide, 76 in tall. Open `van-layout.html` for the three scaled drawings (top-down, passenger wall, driver wall). Distances below are measured from the rear doors unless noted.

## Layout

**Driver side (rear to front)**
- Bed / couch, 0-74 in: 74 x 27 in, folds up. Mattress top about 22 in (16 in platform + 6 in mattress), storage underneath.
- Closet, 74-92.5 in: 18.5 in along the wall, 27 in out from the wall, full height (floor to ceiling). Rod runs front to back (hangers turned sideways): about 27 in of rod, roughly 14-18 garments. Aisle beside it, against the 21 in galley, is 20 in.
- Dinette, 92.5-160 in (67.5 in total), 30 in out from the wall: bench 22.5 in, table 22.5 in (29 in high), bench 22.5 in. Aisle beside it, against the 21 in galley, is 17 in.
- Wall cabinets, 12 in deep, bottom at 56 in: one over the bed (74 in long), one over the dinette (67.5 in long), split by the full-height closet.

**Passenger side (rear to front)**
- Wet bath, 0-36 in: 36 x 24 in, shower curtain as the door (opens onto a 17 in gap in front of the bed).
- Waste tank under the bath: 30 x 15 x 9.5 in (about 15 gal rated). Shower floor sits 9.5 in up, so 66.5 in standing headroom. Toilet seat about 24.5 in on a standard toilet.
- TV (30 in, about 27 x 15.4 in): 36-64 in, bottom at 54 in.
- Countertop at 36 in, 36-123 in, over the cabinet, the fridge, and up to the sink.
- Cabinet / counter, 36-81 in (45 in): water tank on top of the wheel well, under it, microwave on top (18 x 13 x 10.5 in, at 63-81 in).
- Fridge, 81-103 in: 22 in wide, 21 in deep, under-counter (34 in tall or less), right in front of the wheel well.
- Sink, 103-123 in: 20 in wide, 21 in deep.
- Entry, 123-160 in: 37 in clear, behind the passenger seat.
- Wall cabinet, 64-108 in (44 in long), 12 in deep, bottom at 54 in (18 in above the countertop).
- Sliding door: 51 in clear opening, 59 in door panel. Opening drawn at roughly 106-157 in (front edge position still approximate). The panel slides rearward when open, so keep the 59 in track area outside clear.

**Tanks**
- Fresh water: 41 x 10 x 15.5 in, stood on the 10 in side, on top of the passenger wheel well under the counter (38-79 in). Tank bottom sits 7.5 in up, top at 23 in, leaving 13 in under the 36 in countertop. Needs a shelf over the well; the rear 8 in (38-46 in) hangs past the well. Full tank is roughly 27 gal, about 230 lb, so build the shelf strong.
- Waste: see above, under the wet bath. Needs a macerator pump or similar.

## Aisle
20 in aisle between the bed (27 in) and the galley (21 in). 17 in gap between the bath and the bed.

## Measured
- Driver wall pans (recessed steel): ribs between neighbouring pans are 3 in (inside each group); every pan is 25.5 in tall; widths P1 10, P2-P4 10 1/4 (so P3 is 32 3/4 to 43); P1 starts 6 in from the rear doors, P2 19.5 in, P4 46 in, P5 61 in (8 5/8 wide), P6 8 1/4 wide, P7 8 5/8 wide ending 93.25 (ribs between about 3 3/8), P8 104.5, P9 116.25, P10 129, P11 141.25; widths P8 9 1/8, P9 9 1/4, P10 9 1/4, P11 9 1/8 (P11 ends about 150, call it 150); P1 and P11 are angled 1.5 in at the top outer corners. Rest of the pans, holes and seam are placed from photos (see sides.html), about +/- 2 in.
- Sliding door: 51 in opening, 59 in door width.
- Wheel wells: front edge 79 in from the cab, 35 in long, 9 in deep (into the van), 7.5 in tall. Back edge is 46 in from the rear doors.

- The pans (P1-P11) are the body of the van: the other side of the metal is the outside, so a hole through a pan goes through to the outside. The 3 in ribs between pans stand 1/8 in off the body. Standoff of the flat bands, frames, belt line and pillar not measured yet.

## Open items / assumptions to measure
- Sliding door: exact distance from the rear doors to its front edge, and its height (about 58 in assumed).
- Fridge model: needs to be under-counter, 34 in tall or less, with ventilation for being boxed in.
- Bed platform height (16 in assumed) and mattress thickness (6 in assumed).
- Microwave model (18 x 13 x 10.5 in assumed). Needs an inverter of about 2,000 W or more.
- Headroom in the bath is 66.5 in. A taller roof vent dome or pop-up helps if that is too low.
- Standard RV jackknife sofa beds open to about 59-69 in x 42 in, which would block the aisle here. The 74 x 27 fold-up bench keeps the aisle. A rock-and-roll bed was suggested as an alternative.
