Wall marking map: steel, furring strips and items

Diagrams A and D from the static page (the colors are how far the steel stands off the body, the white dots are the furring strips' attachment points) with every built item drawn almost transparent on top, numbered. Each table says where an item sits: measure along the wall from the back mark (0 is 6 in in from the rear-door edge, the same zero as the plan and the wall diagrams, so add 6 in to measure from the doors) and up from the floor. Positions come from the plan and change with its sizes; the steel and the strips are the measured drawing.

The driver wall is drawn with the back of the van on the left. The passenger wall is drawn as diagram D is, with the cab on the left and the back on the right. Dashed item boxes are things that only exist at one height (rods, table tops, the foot ledge): the table gives that one height. Out-from-the-wall depths are not in the tables.
