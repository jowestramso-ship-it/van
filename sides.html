Attachment points. Each white dot on the drawing is a place where the strip is fixed to the van. On S1, S2, S3, S7, and S8 a dot is an adhesive pad (structural adhesive or VHB tape) straight onto the steel, placed on solid steel and kept clear of the holes. On S4 and S6 a dot is a 3 5/8 in spacer block bonded to the 1 1/2 in strip of steel, with the strip screwed to the block. On S5 a dot is a spacer block bonded onto a rib, so S5 touches the van only at the ten ribs, 13 to 16 in apart, and spans the pans between them. This is general van-build practice, so test the adhesive bond on your own steel before you rely on it.

Rough materials, counting 154 in per strip with S1 stopping at the wheel well: about 100 ft of 3/4 x 1 1/2 in strip. Spacers are needed under S4, S5, and S6 at every rib, about 10 per strip. This is a starting layout from your measurements, so check the strip positions against where you want shelves, cabinets, and outlets before you glue anything.

Reading the wall

  • Pans (light grey): eleven pans, each 25.5 in tall (your measurement), from 31 to 56.5 in up (P1's top is 10.5 in below the bottom of T2). The pan is the body of the van; the other side of the metal is the outside, so a hole through a pan goes through to the outside.
  • Ribs (light grey): the 3 in strips between the pans, standing 1/8 in off the body. That is the only standoff measured so far, and it is the shallow space you have behind anything you mount on a rib.
  • Flat steel: a band above the pans (about 56 to 63 in), the belt line below them (about 22 to 31 in, with five oval slots at about 27 in), and the seam pillar at about 98 in. How far these stand off the body is not measured yet; that is the next thing to find.
  • Holes (light grey inside): eight rectangular cutouts and a round hole low on the wall, the pillar opening, and the openings in the roof structure above the wall. These are where the inner steel is missing. The light grey you see in every hole is the outside wall of the van (the body), at the depth listed, the same as the pans. The depth numbers are for the steel around each hole, not inside it: around the top holes (T1-T10 and T8a) it is 3 in from the body at the top edge and 3 3/4 in at the bottom edge; around the L and R holes in the lower wall it is 3 3/4 in; inside every hole you see the body itself. The slots (S), the pillar opening and the low pocket are not measured yet.
