Ford Transit wall diagrams

One diagram per side, to scale (6 px per inch), with a faint grid every 10 inches. The van's steel is drawn here; the built items (bed, closet, dinette, cabinets) are on their own diagram, 1b, further down. Anything measured from the cab end (the wheel well front, P11's end, the cab wall itself) may be off by 2 to 4 in, shown by the hatched band at the right. Reading the wall

  • Pans (dark grey): eleven pans, each 25.5 in tall (your measurement), from 31 to 56.5 in up (P1's top is 10.5 in below the bottom of T2). The pan is the body of the van; the other side of the metal is the outside, so a hole through a pan goes through to the outside.
  • Ribs (light grey): the 3 in strips between the pans, standing 1/8 in off the body. That is the only standoff measured so far, and it is the shallow space you have behind anything you mount on a rib.
  • Flat steel: a band above the pans (about 56 to 63 in), the belt line below them (about 22 to 31 in, with five oval slots at about 27 in), and the seam pillar at about 98 in. How far these stand off the body is not measured yet; that is the next thing to find.
  • Holes (dark grey with an orange edge): eight rectangular cutouts and a round hole low on the wall, the pillar opening, and the openings in the roof structure above the wall. These are where the inner steel is missing. The dark grey you see in every hole is the outside wall of the van (the body), at the depth listed, the same as the pans. The depth numbers are for the steel around each hole, not inside it: around the top holes (T1-T10 and T8a) it is 3 in from the body at the top edge and 3 3/4 in at the bottom edge; around the L and R holes in the lower wall it is 3 3/4 in; inside every hole you see the body itself. The slots (S), the pillar opening and the low pocket are not measured yet.

Side-on cuts through the wall, so you can see how far things stand off the body. Only the numbers you measured are drawn solid. Dashed boxes are parts I don't have a depth for yet. The lower-wall steps below the pans come from your description, so check where the heights fall.

A. Through T3 and pan P3, looking from the rear toward the cab

The pan is the body itself (0 in), so the other side of it is the outside of the van. The top holes are cut in steel that stands 3 to 3 3/4 in off the body, so you can see the body through them. The part between the top of the pan and the bottom of the hole (the flat band and frame, 56 1/2 to 67 in up) has an unknown standoff. Above and below the pans, the first 1 1/2 in (31 to 29 1/2 in up, and 56 1/2 to 58 in up) is 1/8 in off the body, then the wall is 1 1/2 in off the body down to 23 1/4 in up, and under that, all the way to the floor, it is 3 3/4 in off the body (from 15 in from the back to the cab; the bottom 5 in at the back too).
