Reading the wall

  • Pans (blue): eleven pans, each 25.5 in tall (your measurement), from 31 to 56.5 in up (P1's top is 10.5 in below the bottom of T2). The pan is the body of the van; the other side of the metal is the outside, so a hole through a pan goes through to the outside.
  • Ribs (green): the 3 in strips between the pans, standing 1/8 in off the body. That is the only standoff measured so far, and it is the shallow space you have behind anything you mount on a rib.
  • Flat steel: a band above the pans (about 56 to 63 in), the belt line below them (about 22 to 31 in, with five oval slots at about 27 in), and the seam pillar at about 98 in. How far these stand off the body is not measured yet; that is the next thing to find.
  • Holes (dark with orange edge): eight rectangular cutouts and a round hole low on the wall, the pillar opening, and the openings in the roof structure above the rail. These are where the inner steel is missing, so you can see the outer skin or the hollow behind it. How deep each one is has not been measured.
