Attachment points. Each white dot on the drawing is a place where the strip is fixed to the van.   • Ribs (light gray): the 3 in strips between the pans, standing 1/8 in off the body. That is the only standoff measured so far, and it is the shallow space you have behind anything you mount on a rib.
  • Flat steel: a band above the pans (about 56 to 63 in), the belt line below them (about 22 to 31 in, with five oval slots at about 27 in), and the seam pillar at about 98 in. How far these stand off the body is not measured yet; that is the next thing to find.
  • Holes (light gray inside): eight rectangular cutouts and a round hole low on the wall, the pillar opening, and the openings in the roof structure above the wall.
